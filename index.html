Dastaan · Stories from the atelier

From Chowk to your home: three chapters of every piece

Chowk is Lucknow's old city, where the atelier sits. Bhadohi is where the looms are. Every Chaarkone travels between the two before it travels to you.

01

The Loom

It starts with four corners set true on a wooden loom in Bhadohi. Warp threads are strung corner to corner, and the design is chalked onto the grid. If the corners are wrong, nothing that follows can be right.

02

The Hands

Weaving families who have knotted and tufted for generations work the design inward, row by row. A single rug can pass through a dozen pairs of hands before the pattern meets in the middle.

03

The Home

Washed, sun dried, sheared and bound, the piece comes back to Lucknow for finishing, then to your floor. This is the chapter the first two exist for: a room that finally feels like its owner.

Our origin · Meet the founder

Haider Quasim Raza

Chaarkone began with one room. Haider was looking for a carpet for his own space: the right size, the right design, something that felt like him. Custom makers either would not make a single piece, or priced it so high it stopped making sense.

"Why should anyone have to compromise when it comes to their home?"

That question became the atelier. Today Chaarkone designs in Lucknow and crafts in Bhadohi, bringing together Lucknow's artistic soul and Bhadohi's carpet craftsmanship, one commissioned piece at a time.

Why "Chaarkone"?

Chaarkone is Hindi for "four corners", a name for the loom itself. Every rug begins and ends at four corners, worked inward by hand until they meet. The name is the method.

Our craft

Three weaves, one standard: by hand

Lucknow's artistic soul meets Bhadohi's carpet craftsmanship. The same hands, the same looms, the same patience.

Hand Knotted The heirloom weave · 3 to 6 months

Each knot tied and cut by hand on a vertical loom, up to a million knots in a large rug. The densest, longest lived weave we make.

Hand Tufted The expressive weave · 4 to 6 weeks

The design is drawn on canvas, wool is punched through with a hand tool, and the pile is sheared to relief. Every collection in the shop above is hand tufted.

Hand Loomed The quiet weave · 3 to 5 weeks

Shuttle looms and flat weaves for solids and texture. The fastest of the three, and the calmest thing in a room.
